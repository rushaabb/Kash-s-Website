About

Kashmira is a versatile vocalist and singer-songwriter from Pune’s vibrant independent music scene, with over seven years of experience performing and creating music. Known for her dynamic stage presence and distinctive vocal style, she has captivated audiences both as a solo artist and as the frontwoman of rock bands Nine Lives Club and Stardust Symphony.

With a foundation in Indian classical music, Kashmira blends pop, rock, grunge, and classic sounds into her distinctive style. Passionate about original music, she has released five singles and an EP, Now Then, earning features across publications including Rolling Stone India and Indian Music Diaries, as well as radio stations such as Radio One, Radio City, BIG FM, and HUGS Radio-USA.
